SBA-Certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business · Set-Aside Eligible
What applies. Section 508 covers the ICT federal agencies develop, procure, maintain, or use. It has no compliance date: it is in force now, and a VPAT is due with the bid.

A veteran-owned consultancy that establishes where an organization stands against Section 508 and produces what a contracting officer asks for: an Accessibility Conformance Report in VPAT 2.5 format, every criterion marked and evidenced. Delivered by the principal, a U.S. Navy veteran who founded and led three enterprise AI, data and analytics practices.

Who we serve: federal agencies, and the vendors selling ICT to them who owe a VPAT with the bid.

Core Competencies

  • Section 508 Conformance Assessment. Keyboard, screen reader, and automated testing, delivered as a VPAT 2.5 ACR and a roadmap.
  • Vendor VPAT for a Bid. An independent ACR for the product a solicitation names, delivered by the bid date.
  • Document Inventory & Triage. Every PDF sorted into retire, replace, remediate, or leave, delivered as a biddable work order.

Differentiators

  • Evaluation only, never the remediation: no self-assessment.
  • A tagged PDF is not a conforming PDF: veraPDF checks every one.
  • SDVOSB set-aside eligible: SBA-certified, active in SAM.gov.
  • Triage before spend: remediation is priced per page, so sorting obsolete, duplicate, and superseded documents first is the cheapest money in the program.
  • No product to sell: no overlay, no platform license, no per-page quota.

Past Performance

  • brianbeals.com: 38 pages, 4 PDF/UA-1 documents, all 50 criteria.
  • ACR, Oct. 17, 2026: no Partially Supports or Does Not Support.
  • Volume screening: 22 Florida public entities sampled across four counties, findings mapped to specific success criteria.
