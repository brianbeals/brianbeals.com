Two obligations. SF&P §3.5-311 puts a nonvisual access warranty in state ICT contracts, with DoIT review within 18 months of award. Title II requires WCAG 2.1 AA by April 26, 2027 or April 26, 2028.

A veteran-owned consultancy that establishes where an agency or a prime stands against Maryland's nonvisual access requirement and the Title II deadline, in an Accessibility Conformance Report that marks and evidences every WCAG 2.1 A and AA criterion. Delivered by the principal, a U.S. Navy veteran who founded and led three enterprise AI, data and analytics practices.

Who we serve: Maryland agencies, counties, and colleges, and the primes and CATS+ masters bidding to them.

Core Competencies

  • Nonvisual Access Conformance Evidence. Evaluation against COMAR 14.33.02 and WCAG 2.1 AA for DoIT's post-award review.
  • Title II Readiness Assessment. Keyboard, screen reader, and automated testing, delivered as a VPAT 2.5 ACR and a roadmap.
  • Document Inventory & Triage. Every PDF sorted into retire, replace, remediate, or leave, delivered as a biddable work order.
  • Program & Policy Setup. Accessibility statement, coordinator designation, grievance procedure, and procurement language requiring a VPAT or ACR before purchase.

Differentiators

  • Maryland's rule is older: in state IT contracts since 2005.
  • Evaluation only, never the remediation: no self-assessment.
  • A tagged PDF is not a conforming PDF: veraPDF checks every one.
  • No product to sell: no overlay, no platform license, no per-page quota.
