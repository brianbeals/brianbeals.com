Changes since the prior report

This report is a re-evaluation. The prior report is dated October 11, 2026, published at https://brianbeals.com/conformance-report-2026-10-11b.html. Every page and document below was evaluated again against the site as it stood on October 17, 2026.

  • 34 pages were evaluated in the prior report and 38 in this one.
  • Pages added since the prior report: /accessibility/before-we-start, /accessibility/federal, /accessibility/pricing, /accessibility/title-ii, /scanner, /writing/the-form-that-sets-the-price.
  • Pages no longer found: /capability-statement-state-local.html, /capability-statement.html.
  • 4 of the 4 published documents were revised after the prior scan and were validated again: Accessibility practice, ADA Title II; Accessibility practice, federal Section 508; Accessibility practice, Section 504 healthcare; Accessibility practice, Maryland CATS+.
  • Document no longer linked: Federal, SDVOSB.
  • Document no longer linked: Florida state and local, VBE.
  • The five added pages went up on these dates: /accessibility/federal on October 12, /accessibility/pricing and /accessibility/before-we-start on October 15, /accessibility/title-ii, the ADA Title II program page, on October 16, and /scanner, About our scanners, the page the firm's two scanners link to from their User-Agents, on October 17. Four criteria that had no form to judge now do: 1.3.5, 3.2.2, 3.3.1 and 3.3.3 move from Not Applicable to Supports, after a keyboard pass over each form's empty, corrected and sent states.
  • On October 16 the site header became a navy bar with the firm name, the navigation and a call link, pinned to the top of wide windows and of phones held upright, and scrolling away in landscape and at high zoom, with the navigation under a Menu button below 1024 pixels; the homepage was rewritten, its three service cards each a single link covering the card; every page heading moved onto shared components and one type scale; and the light-mode error red darkened from #C0392B to #A93226, which measured 4.21:1 at the darkest point of the page background and now measures 5.14:1. /about was rewritten to lead with the accessibility practice, and four essays changed bylines.   • /tools/account-intel was retired on October 15. The private area is now /desk, behind the same sign-in, and the scope line names it.
  • The second October 11 report moves to /conformance-report-2026-10-11b.html so this report's citation resolves, and is listed in both site maps.
  • On October 17 the sentence period moved outside the bold run in six labels on /accessibility/federal, /accessibility/before-we-start and the two forms' error messages, where VoiceOver on macOS 27 had spelled the next word letter by letter.
  • On October 17 the error summary on both forms was rebuilt after a screen reader listening pass: focus now moves to a "There are N problems" heading that names every error, and each error is a link naming the field and the fix that moves focus to it. The homepage's trust strip became a list of four items, and every page title other than an essay's now ends "| Brian Beals, LLC".
