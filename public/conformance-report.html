Applicable standards

WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA, all 50 criteria. Section 508 incorporates WCAG 2.0 Level AA, a strict subset, so conformance below satisfies it. ADA Title II adopts WCAG 2.1 Level AA directly. Level AAA was not evaluated.

Limitation on assistive technology testing. This evaluation did not include screen reader testing. Criteria that depend on assistive technology behavior (4.1.2) were assessed by inspecting the browser accessibility tree and observing the DOM conditions that determine whether a change is announced. Those conditions were satisfied, which is a strong indicator and not the same as hearing the announcement.
