Changes since the prior report

This report is a re-evaluation. The prior report is dated October 17, 2026, published at https://brianbeals.com/conformance-report-2026-10-17.html. Every page and document below was evaluated again against the site as it stood on October 22, 2026.

What changed on the site

  • 38 pages were evaluated in the prior report and 39 in this one.
  • Pages added since the prior report: /free-test.
  • 4 of the 4 published documents were revised after the prior scan and were validated again: Accessibility practice, ADA Title II; Accessibility practice, federal Section 508; Accessibility practice, Section 504 healthcare; Accessibility practice, Maryland CATS+.
