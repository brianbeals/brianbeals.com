2. Career Background

▾

Twelve years inside IBM (1999–2011) form the spine of his career: global desktop and software-distribution delivery for American Express as an outsourcing provider, high-performance compute for portfolio managers and traders, a Client IT Architect role responsible for over $200M in annual revenue in Retail and Consumer Products, and an internal strategy consultant role advising senior IBM executives on a business-analytics growth plan that reached the chairman and executive committee.

Then about four years at CA Technologies as Executive Advisor and Business Solution Architect, running CIO and VP-level workshops across Midwest industries and building an internal architect community. At Evolving Solutions since 2014, where the architect-to-advisor-to-CTO arc completed.

Career arc summary: a builder who rose by translating technology trends into business-model language for senior leaders, with an unusual origin (music degree before a full technology career).

3. Likely Priorities & Pain Points

▾

Inferred from his role and company content.

Operationalizing AI past the pilot stage

Evolving Solutions has built an "Operationalizing AI" solution line and an "AI and Intelligent Operations" capability, and publishes material like "AI at Enterprise Scale: The Infrastructure Behind the Model." A recurring theme in their content: organizations see early AI wins but struggle to turn pilots into measurable results. As technology owner, the data and infrastructure foundation under AI is squarely his territory.

Observability that keeps up with changing systems

Observability is a named practice; a June 2026 company blog covers observability debt, agentic AI in operations, and decision-level telemetry, tied to the Observability Summit held in Minneapolis (May 21–22, 2026). The shift from dashboards toward self-healing, AI-assisted operations is an active company narrative.

Platform modernization and technical debt without rip-and-replace

Current solution lines include Platform Modernization and Technical Debt Remediation, plus content on modernizing IBM Power environments without replatforming and rethinking virtualization after the VMware licensing changes.

Cloud and AI cost control (FinOps)

The company eBook "Get Control of IT Spend" argues FinOps has moved beyond cloud-waste visibility to governing the cost of running AI at scale.
